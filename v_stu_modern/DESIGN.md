## Brand & Style
The design system powers an institutional-grade, modern Indian property marketplace. It replaces the chaotic, ad-dense visual fatigue typical of legacy real estate portals with the quiet confidence, architectural clarity, and utilitarian rigor of high-end SaaS applications.

### Design Principles
- **Clarity over Density**: Complex property data (carpet area, super built-up, RERA registration, floor configuration) must be legible at a glance without clutter. High structural whitespace offsets information richness.
- **Uncompromised Legitimacy & Trust**: Verification, verified seller badges, pricing trends, and registry details are treated with the precision of financial interfaces.
- **Modern Architectural Balance**: Drawing inspiration from contemporary Indian urban architecture, the UI uses warm slate tones, crisp geometric dividers, and measured saffron-coral energetic highlights for vital calls-to-action.

### Design Style
The system adopts **Corporate Modernism with Tactile SaaS Precision**: crisp hairline borders (`1px` solid slate), subtle ambient warm-tinted elevation tiers, and refined typographic contrast that makes dense listings feel effortless to scan on mobile screens in transit or desktop widescreen viewports.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative institutional blue with energetic saffron-terracotta accents and crisp foundational slates.

### Functional Palette Mapping
- **Primary (`#1E3A8A`) — Deep Royal Indigo**: Primary buttons, active navigation states, primary branding moments, selected tab indicators, and RERA verification headers. Communicates institutional trust, banking-grade security, and long-term value.
- **Secondary (`#EA580C`) — Vibrant Indian Terracotta / Warm Saffron**: High-priority conversion drivers, "Contact Owner/Agent" CTAs, exclusive property tags, and interactive map pins.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`) — Verified Emerald**: RERA-approved status chips, verified seller markers, zero-brokerage callouts, and positive appreciation percentage indicators.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`) — Slate Obsidian**: Primary typography, high-contrast headings, and crisp vector iconography.
- **Neutral Muted (`#475569` to `#64748B`)**: Secondary listing metadata (furnishing status, possession dates, locality details).
- **Surface Foundations**:
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (Warm slate-white ground)
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white card containers)
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F1F5F9` (Subtle filter panels, chip backgrounds, disabled surfaces)
  - `Border Neutral`: `#E2E8F0` (Default hairline container dividers)
  - `Border Active`: `#CBD5E1` (Input borders, hovered states)

## Typography
`Plus Jakarta Sans` acts as the unified geometric foundation across display, body, and UI labels. Its wide aperture, contemporary geometric styling, and clean numerical glyphs provide pristine rendering for dense Indian numeric expressions.

### Numeric & Currency Formatting Guidelines
- **Indian Rupee Symbol (`₹`)**: Always set with an explicit zero-width or hairline space between the glyph and the digits (e.g., `₹ 1.85 Cr`, `₹ 45,000/mo`).
- **Denominations**: Use standard Indian numerical naming rules with standardized capitalization:
  - `Lakh` or `L` (e.g., `₹ 75 L`) for amounts between 1,00,000 and 99,99,999.
  - `Crore` or `Cr` (e.g., `₹ 2.45 Cr`) for amounts of 1,00,00,000 and above.
  - Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be enabled on property comparison matrices, price breakdowns, and EMI calculator summaries.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid anchored by an 8-point vertical spacing rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 640px`)**: 4-column layout with `margin-mobile` (16px) and `gutter-mobile` (12px). Sticky bottom action bars for immediate direct-contact engagement.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins and 16px gutters. Listing views support split map/list toggling.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`)**: 12-column container with a max content width of `1320px`, 32px canvas margins, and 24px gutters. Provides a persistent 360px sticky filter/search rail alongside a 2-column or 3-column listing feed.

### Spacing Usage
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between icons and adjacent labels; tag inline padding.
- `space-sm` (8px): Stack spacing between secondary metadata rows and badge collections.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for compact card components, form controls, and search chips.
- `space-lg` (24px): Primary padding within detailed listing cards, drawer sheets, and modal windows.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section block margins separating related localities, floor plans, and amenities grids.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system avoids generic grey box-shadows in favor of a layered hierarchy utilizing warm slate-tinted ambient illumination and clean 1px hairline borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Used on `#F8FAFC` canvas surfaces with no shadow. Structural dividers use `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0` with a subtle diffuse tint:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Filter Floating)**: Activated property cards and sticky navigation bars:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Lead Dialogs & Floating Search Bars)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Glassmorphism Accent**: Sticky mobile filter navigation and image counter badges leverage a frosted slate wash: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

## Shapes
The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** shape language, communicating architectural precision, structural reliability, and technical competence.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Base Components (`rounded`)**: `0.25rem` (4px). Checkboxes, small tag badges, micro buttons.
- **Standard UI Elements (`rounded-md`)**: `0.375rem` (6px). Form inputs, dropdown menus, button components.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: `0.5rem` (8px). Property cards, filter dialogs, interactive map cards.
- **High-Level Surfaces (`rounded-xl`)**: `0.75rem` (12px). Main hero search containers and booking/inquiry modals.
- **Pill Exception**: Filter quick-toggle chips and badge indicators use `rounded-full` (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual distinction between actionable filters and structured information cards.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#EA580C`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, `rounded-md`. Hover: `#C2410C`. Focus: 2px ring offset with `#EA580C`. Reserved for direct lead actions: "Contact Owner", "Book Site Visit".
- **Secondary Corporate**: Background `#1E3A8A`, text `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-md`. Reserved for institutional actions: "Download Brochure", "Check Home Loan Eligibility".
- **Outline / Ghost**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`.

### 2. Badges & Property Trust Tokens
All badges use uppercase typography with `label-sm` tracking (`+0.04em`), `px-2 py-0.5`, `rounded-full`.
- **Verified**: `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` text, leading checkmark shield icon.
- **RERA Approved**: `#EFF6FF` background, `#1D4ED8` text, official badge iconography.
- **Featured**: `#FFF7ED` background, `#C2410C` text.
- **Owner Listing (Zero Brokerage)**: `#F5F3FF` background, `#6D28D9` text.
- **Agent / Builder**: `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text.

### 3. Property Cards
- Built on `Level 1` elevation with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and `rounded-lg` overflow-hidden clipping.
- **Image Gallery Aspect**: 16:10 ratio with bottom-right floating frosted counter (`1/14 Photos`).
- **Price Block**: Emphasized using `price-hero` styling in `#0F172A`, directly followed by per-sq-ft calculation (`₹ 8,450 / sq.ft`) in muted slate.
- **Specification Row**: Dedicated 3-column micro-grid showcasing:
  - Configuration (e.g., `3 BHK`)
  - Super Built-up / Carpet Area (e.g., `1,650 sq.ft`)
  - Status (e.g., `Ready to Move` or `Possession Dec 2026`)
- **Card Footer**: Clear demarcation separating seller persona ("Direct Owner" vs "Premium Broker") and dual-action triggers: "View Phone" (Outline) & "Chat / WhatsApp" (Primary or Emerald accent).

### 4. Search & Filter System
- **Hero Search Bar**: Elevated horizontal segmented pill container. Combines: Buy/Rent tabs, City/Locality auto-suggest with landmark detection, Property Type multi-select, and Budget range sliders.
- **Quick-Pills**: Horizontal scrolling filter chips (`rounded-full`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`). Active state: background `#1E3A8A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Examples: "Ready to Move", "Under Construction", "Verified Only", "Gated Community".

### 5. Form Inputs & Budget Selectors
- Height standard: `44px` for touch targets.
- Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, internal padding `0 14px`.
- Dual-thumb range sliders for Indian price brackets with labeled ticks: `₹ 25 L`, `₹ 50 L`, `₹ 1 Cr`, `₹ 2 Cr`, `₹ 5 Cr+`.
